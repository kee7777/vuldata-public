The focus of this study is to find how to best leverage small amount of human labeled data in pursuit of improving software vulnerability detection and reducing costs. Our method employs the use of two different models, therefore, it is crucial that we also determine why these models result in different subsets, doing so will allow us to determine how to fine tune each model. We also aim to measure the performance of models in reaction to small numbers of human labeled data (50-100), to identify if they have meaningful impact. 

This research will focus on answering these two questions:

    1. What differs between the two resulting sets of commit patches from the UQ model and the LLM model?


    2. What amount of improvement towards vulnerability prediction is increased as human data points are added?
